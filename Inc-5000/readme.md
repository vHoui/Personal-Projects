# Project Description:

Inc. 5000 is a dataset containing information about each company growth accomplishments in 2019. Fields include the company name, industry, founding year, website, location, 2019 revenue, % growth, number of workers (year-over-year), and the number of years on the list.

*** Independent/Group: *** This was done as a group project. I worked with 2 other business major students at my University at Wright State.
